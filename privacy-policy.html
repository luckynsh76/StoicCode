MoneyGuardian Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

MoneyGuardian ("MoneyGuardian", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, protected, and shared when you use the MoneyGuardian mobile application.

Important: MoneyGuardian is a personal finance and budgeting application. Information you enter into the app may include financial information, such as income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, and spending categories. We treat this information as private and do not publicly disclose your financial information.

1. Information You Provide

MoneyGuardian allows you to enter information to help you manage your personal finances. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Income amounts and income-related information
  • Expenses and spending amounts
  • Budget amounts and budget categories
  • Custom category names
  • Savings and financial goals
  • Purchase decision information
  • Currency preferences
  • Financial notes or descriptions you choose to enter
  • App preferences and settings

You are responsible for ensuring that information you enter into MoneyGuardian is accurate and that you do not enter information that you do not have permission to use.

2. Financial Information

MoneyGuardian is designed to help you organize and understand your personal finances. Financial information entered into the application is used to provide budgeting, expense tracking, financial analysis, reports, and related functionality.

MoneyGuardian does not sell your personal financial information.

We do not publicly display your financial information or make your financial information available to other users.

MoneyGuardian does not provide banking, investment, lending, tax, accounting, or financial-advisory services. Information and calculations provided by the application are intended for personal educational and organizational purposes.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain information used by MoneyGuardian may be stored locally on your device so that the application can provide its features and remember your preferences.

This may include financial entries, budgets, categories, reports, currency preferences, application settings, and security preferences.

If you uninstall the application, locally stored application data may be removed by the operating system. Data stored through other services may be subject to the retention policies of those services.

4. App Security and Authentication

MoneyGuardian may provide security features such as PIN protection, device authentication, and biometric authentication.

When biometric authentication is used, MoneyGuardian relies on the authentication mechanisms provided by your Android device and operating system. MoneyGuardian does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial biometric template, or other biometric data.

Security information is used to protect access to the application and its protected features.

5. Analytics and App Usage Information

MoneyGuardian may use analytics technologies to understand how the application is used and to improve performance, reliability, and user experience.

Depending on the services enabled in the application, analytics information may include:

  • App launches and usage events
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Application version
  • Performance and diagnostic information
  • Crash and error information
  • General usage statistics

Analytics information is used to understand application performance, identify technical problems, and improve MoneyGuardian.

6. Advertising

MoneyGuardian may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising identifiers, application information, and advertising interaction information, subject to the provider's policies and applicable privacy choices.

Advertising information may be used to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements.

MoneyGuardian does not intentionally send the financial information you enter into your budgets or expenses to advertisers for the purpose of identifying you or targeting advertisements based on your personal financial information.

Google may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and applicable advertising technologies.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google requires appropriate disclosures and consent mechanisms for personalized advertising where applicable.

MoneyGuardian may use Google's consent management technologies to provide applicable privacy choices.

Google's privacy and consent requirements may change over time, and advertising services may process information according to applicable laws and Google's policies.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment card numbers and other payment credentials are handled by Google and the applicable payment provider rather than being stored by MoneyGuardian.

MoneyGuardian may receive information necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is active, such as purchase status, subscription status, product information, and transaction-related identifiers provided by Google Play.

Google Play's own terms and privacy policies apply to payment processing.

These services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Third-party services used by MoneyGuardian may include services provided by:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing

We do not sell your personal information.
